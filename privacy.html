Privacy Policy for ParserPro

ParserPro is a local-first household receipt scanner and expenditure tracker. You do not need an account to use the app, your saved receipts remain stored locally on your own device, and we never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data.

01. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how ParserPro (Android package identifier com.receiptscannerpro.app, also accessible as a web application) handles information when you photograph receipts, extract line items, track monthly household budgets, and export backups.

ParserPro is designed around a local-first architecture: we do not operate user accounts, cloud receipt databases, advertising SDKs, or cross-app behavioral trackers.

02. Android Permissions We Request

ParserPro requests only the Android system permissions strictly required for scanning receipts and exporting your backups:

Permission Purpose When Triggered
android.permission.CAMERA Captures photos of paper receipts, slips, and utility invoices for item extraction. Only when you tap the Scan / Camera button. Never accessed in the background.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 & below, maxSdkVersion="32") Allows importing receipt photos from your gallery and saving JSON, CSV, or PDF expenditure backups to your device Downloads or Documents folder. Only when you explicitly import a file or export a backup report.
android.permission.INTERNET Transmits a receipt image over encrypted HTTPS when cloud OCR extraction is selected. Only during active receipt extraction when online OCR mode is enabled.

You may revoke camera or storage permissions at any time in your Android system settings. ParserPro continues to support manual receipt entry and offline local viewing even if camera permission is declined.

03. Receipt Scanning & Image Processing

ParserPro offers two methods for extracting text (vendor name, invoice date, line items, VAT/tax, and total amount) from receipt images:

  • Encrypted Cloud OCR Extraction: When online extraction is used, the receipt photo you select or capture is transmitted over TLS/HTTPS to our stateless extraction endpoint solely to parse structured receipt text and return JSON line items back to your device. Receipt images are processed transiently in memory (memoryStorage) and are not stored, logged, or retained on our servers after the response is returned.
  • On-Device Offline OCR: When offline OCR mode is selected in Settings, optical character recognition runs locally on your device without transmitting the receipt photo over the network.

Please avoid photographing sensitive unrelated documents (such as identity cards, passports, or full payment card numbers). Standard retail receipts typically mask card digits automatically.

04. Local On-Device Data Storage

All extracted receipts, edited line items, monthly category budgets, and application preferences are stored exclusively on your own device using Android WebView / browser local storage technologies (IndexedDB and localStorage).

  • No Cloud Account Database: We do not maintain a central database of your household purchases, vendor history, or monthly expenditure totals.
  • User-Controlled Backups: When you use the Backup (.json), CSV Ledger (.csv), or Printable Report features, files are saved directly to your device storage or shared only through the Android share target you explicitly choose.

05. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or receipt history with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics networks.

  • Zero Advertising & Analytics SDKs: ParserPro contains no third-party ad networks, marketing pixels, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • OCR Infrastructure Providers: When cloud OCR mode is active, receipt image bytes are processed through encrypted API infrastructure strictly to return structured receipt fields and are never used by us to build user profiles.
  • Legal Compliance: Because we do not store your receipt database on our servers, we do not possess historical user expenditure records to disclose to third parties.

06. Data Retention & Instant Deletion

Because your expenditure records live locally on your device, you have complete, immediate control over retention and deletion at all times:

  • Delete Individual Receipts or Months: Delete any single invoice or clear an entire month directly from the Receipts or Settings tab.
  • Instant Full Data Wipe: Open Settings → Reset All Data inside ParserPro to permanently erase all saved invoices, line items, and totals from your device in one tap.
  • Uninstall Cleanup: Uninstalling ParserPro or clearing app storage in Android Settings permanently removes all local app databases from your phone.

07. Children's Privacy

ParserPro is a general-audience household budgeting and utility tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us via email support, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

08. Your Privacy Rights & Contact Information

Depending on your location—including under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the European Union / UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and California privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA)—you have the right to know how your data is processed, export your data in portable formats (JSON and CSV built into the app), and erase your data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or Google Play policy updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
