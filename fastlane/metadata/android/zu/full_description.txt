I-ParserPro iguqula amarisidi aseveki nama-invoyisi abe amarekhodi ezindleko zasendlini ahlelekile ngemizuzwana.

IZICI EZIYINHLOKO
• Ukuskena Amarisidi Okuhlakaniphile (OCR): Thatha isithombe serisidi noma i-invoyisi ukuze ukhiphe igama lesitolo, usuku, intela, inani eliphelele kanye nezinto ozithengile.
• Isebenza Ngaphandle Kwe-inthanethi: Gcina amarisidi ohlwini olungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi noma uwacubungule khona manjalo efonini yakho.
• Izabelomali Zenyanga: Lawula izindleko zegrosa, ugesi namanzi, uphethiloli kanye nezidingo zasekhaya ngezigaba zenyanga.
• Ukusesha Nokuhlela Kalula: Sesha okuthengile ngesitolo, usuku noma igama lento.
• Ubumfihlo Obuphelele: Amarekhodi akho agcinwa kudivayisi yakho ngaphandle kwezikhangiso. Khipha isipele se-CSV noma se-JSON noma nini.
