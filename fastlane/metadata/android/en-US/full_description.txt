ParserPro turns paper receipts and digital invoices into clean, organized household expense records in seconds.

KEY FEATURES
• Smart Receipt & Invoice OCR: Snap a photo or import a receipt image to automatically extract store names, dates, totals, taxes, and individual line items.
• Offline On-Device Queue: No signal in the store? Queue receipts offline or process them with on-device OCR so you never lose a slip.
• Monthly Spending & Category Budgets: Track groceries, utilities, fuel, dining, healthcare, and household spending with clear monthly limits and visual progress.
• Line-Item Search & Editing: Search past purchases by store, date, or item description to check price history and edit any entry anytime.
• Private Local-First Storage: Your financial records stay stored locally on your device. Export full backups to CSV spreadsheets or JSON files whenever you need them.

Built for everyday household budgeting, expense tracking, and stress-free receipt organization—with zero ads and zero tracking SDKs.
